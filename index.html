Start with the monthly number

A useful retirement benchmark is about $1,384 per person a month.

The closest retirement-specific national benchmark is from households made up only of non-employed people aged 65+. They averaged $2,349 per household per month in 2023, or $1,384 per household member. It is a starting point, not a personal target.

How do Endowus / Syfe payouts compare with CPF LIFE?

CPF LIFE: lifelong payout with CPF rules and plan choices. Endowus / Syfe investment payouts: distributions can vary, portfolio values can fall, and a distribution is not the same as a guaranteed return. Compare fees, payout source, capital impact and what happens in a market downturn.

THE 1M65 IDEA

A milestone, not a required target.

“1M65” describes one possible CPF accumulation journey. Your useful target is the monthly income your own budget needs. At 55, CPF creates an RA and transfers savings under the rules for your cohort; check your own forecast and housing needs before voluntary top-ups or transfers.

Make investments easier to spend later

A drawdown plan for the years after work.

If you hold a broad share fund such as VWRA, build a written transition plan over time. A retirement date does not require selling everything at once.

01

Calculate the gap first

Use the monthly plan above. Size investments against spending left after CPF LIFE and any other steady income.

02

Set an accessible spending reserve

Choose how many months of your investment-funded gap to hold in cash or similarly accessible savings, based on comfort and upcoming costs.

03

Build a fixed-income ladder gradually

Match maturities to planned spending. Singapore Savings Bonds can be redeemed monthly without a penalty; allow for processing time, so they are not instant cash.

04

Keep long-horizon money invested

Keep an equity allocation you can tolerate through market falls. Rebalance on a set schedule and use a written rule for replenishing cash after stronger markets.

Buy in $500 multiples. You can request redemption monthly, with no penalty; proceeds are paid after processing in the following month. Keep near-term bills in a more immediately accessible account.
