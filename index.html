Before you commit

Four gotchas to keep in view

  • CPF top-ups are generally irreversible. Check liquidity, housing plans and eligibility first.
  • Not all CPF balances earn the same rate. Account rules and extra interest matter.
  • A payout is not automatically a return. Check guarantees, duration, fees and capital.
  • Early exit can cost you. Surrender value may be below premiums paid, especially early on.
Check CPF details →

“Max CPF first” can be a useful question, but it is not a universal rule. CPF top-ups and transfers can restrict access; secure your near-term needs and compare your personal options first.

Investment-linked policy02

Any ILP, including retirement-branded plans

An ILP combines insurance with investments in sub-funds. The value can move with markets and charges may be taken from units over time.

In plain English

“You are buying a policy plus an investment account. You carry the investment risk.”

BEFORE YOU SIGN

Seven questions that cut through the pitch.

  1. 01

    Which benefits are guaranteed, and which are only illustrated?

  2. 02

    What is the total premium I will pay before the first payout?

  3. 03

    Show me the cash / surrender value at years 1, 3, 5, 10 and after the lock-in.

  4. 04

    What happens if I stop paying, pause, or need a partial withdrawal?

  5. 05

    How much of my premium buys units or funds, and what charges are taken?

  6. 06

    What is the comparable simpler or more liquid alternative?

  7. 07

    Can I take every document home and come back after I have compared it?

A KEY CPF MILESTONE

What changes at 55?

A Retirement Account is created. Savings move from SA first, then OA, up to the applicable Full Retirement Sum. Your own cohort’s FRS is set when you turn 55; the 2026 figure above is for that cohort only.

OA + SA→Retirement Account→CPF LIFE payouts

Pause before topping up: CPF cash top-ups and transfers to RA are generally irreversible and reserved for retirement payouts. Check housing plans, emergency cash and eligibility first.

Income tools, with the fine print visible

A payout button does not make income guaranteed.

Endowus and Syfe can route investment distributions to a bank account. The amount can change, the fund value can fall, and a payout may include money that could otherwise stay invested.

Lifetime income

CPF LIFE

Government-backed lifelong payouts based on your CPF savings, start age and chosen plan.

  • Payouts continue for life.
  • Escalating plan starts lower and grows 2% yearly.
  • Best used as a baseline for essential recurring expenses.

Investment distributions

Endowus Income

Income portfolios use investment funds and show a current payout target. Distributions depend on fund holdings and are not guaranteed.

  • Check the live target, fees and portfolio risk.
  • Cash, CPF and SRS payout handling can differ by portfolio.
  • A distribution is not the same thing as total investment return.

Syfe Income+

Selected portfolios offer monthly distribution payout settings. Payouts and portfolio value can vary with markets.

Make future-you’s life easier

Set up an exit system before it gets hard to track.

The safest retirement plan is one you, or someone you trust, can understand later. Keep a short inventory and a repeatable review date.

ONE PAGE+ONE TRUSTED PERSON+ONE REVIEW DATE

Annual “still right for me?” check

  1. 1

    List every policy, CPF LIFE plan, investment account and bank account.

  2. 2

    Write down the provider, purpose, current value, payout, fees and withdrawal steps.

  3. 3

    Store statements and contact details securely. Do not put passwords or OTPs in the list.

  4. 4

    Choose a trusted person who knows where the list is and how to contact providers.

  5. 5

    Review beneficiaries, CPF nomination, bank details and recurring transfers after major life changes.

Never share Singpass credentials, passwords or one-time codes. Use official provider contact channels.
