      What the calculator models (and excludes)

      Money basis & timeline
      Starts Dec 2026 through age 90. Displayed outputs use today’s purchasing power (3% default inflation); monthly savings and salary stay level in cash with no bonuses.
      CPF interest & extra tiers
      OA 2.5%; SA/MA/RA 4% p.a. (computed monthly, credited annually). Extra interest: +1% on first $60k (<55) or +2% first $30k / +1% next $30k (55+), max $20k from OA.
      Age-55 SA closure & caps
      SA closes at 55; SA then OA fund RA up to cohort FRS (2026: $220.4k; 2027: $228.2k; +3.5%/yr assumed after). 2026 BHS is $79k (+5%/yr assumed), overflowing to SA/RA/OA.
      CPF LIFE payout tables
      Interpolated from published 2026 male-member RA-at-65 anchors starting at 65. Standard is flat in cash; Escalating starts ~20% lower and rises 2%/yr.
      Lifestyle & care trend
      CCA starts at $2,500/mo ($3,340 at 75 in today’s dollars); apartment lease and move-in deposits are excluded.
      Returns & exclusions
      Default 4% net nominal portfolio return (constant, no crash sequence). Blank housing assumes new OA pays housing; flat sale proceeds and subsidies stay excluded.
