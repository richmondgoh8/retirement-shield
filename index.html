Retirement budget

What preset budgets include

Both presets reflect typical Singapore living costs: $800/mo for food and dining, AIA HealthShield Gold Max A with VitalHealth Pro A ($153/mo at age 35: $86 cash + $67 MediSave), GE Supreme Multiplier Life with personal accident and term cover, HDB fire and home insurance, utilities, fibre broadband and streaming, daily supplements ($35/mo), home maintenance and aircon servicing ($120–$150/mo), and an annual travel and contingency reserve ($410–$540/mo), plus either the CCA Basic Service Package ($175/mo) or a live-in helper ($1,230/mo including levy). Projections automatically step up HealthShield Max A + VitalHealth Pro A across older age brackets ($153/mo at 35 → $621/mo at 65 → $1,216/mo at 75 → $1,730/mo at 85 in today’s $) while GE Supreme Multiplier and term/personal accident cover finish by retirement.
