# CPF & Retirement Model Notes

Employee planning model for Singapore citizens and full-rate PRs (starting ages 18–54). All calculations run in the browser; figures are planning illustrations, not personalised CPF Board quotes.

## Core Mechanics

- **Timeline:** Projections start December 2026 (`C.startMonth = 11`); entered age is treated as exact then.
- **CPF interest:** Base and extra interest are computed monthly on eligible balances (without monthly compounding) and credited after December. New contributions earn interest from the following month; deductions lose interest in the deduction month.
- **Extra interest tiers:** Applied in priority order `RA → OA (max $20,000) → SA → MA`, sharing a $60,000 combined cap (negative balances clamped to `$0`). OA extra interest goes to SA before 55 and RA from 55. From 55, the first $30,000 earns +2% and the next $30,000 earns +1%.
- **Age-55 SA closure & Age-65 RA settlement:** SA and OA fund RA up to the cohort Full Retirement Sum (FRS), or Basic Retirement Sum (BRS) under Property Pledge, or Enhanced Retirement Sum (ERS) when selected. Pre-closure SA interest credits to RA up to the post-55 RA cap, then OA. Between ages 55 and 65, post-55 excess OA withdrawals prorate foregone 2.5% OA interest by remaining months in the withdrawal year (`(12 - monthInYear) / 12`). At age 65, final accrued CPF interest (January–November before payout start) is settled into balances before sweeping eligible OA up to the RA requirement; if post-55 excess OA withdrawals reduced OA below the planned age-65 sweep, CPF LIFE payouts scale down accordingly without double-counting OA.
- **MediSave, BHS & post-65 CPF LIFE bequest:** 2026 BHS is $79,000 (`C.bhs`, assumed 5% growth thereafter; fixed for life once a cohort turns 65). Pre-65 overflow above BHS routes to SA/RA up to cohort limits, then OA; post-65 MA stays capped at the cohort's fixed age-65 BHS. Post-65 CPF LIFE remaining bequest tracks initial RA premium committed at 65 minus cumulative payouts received, while monthly CPF LIFE income continues for life.
- **Top-up limits, VC-3A & outside reserve:** Pre-55 cash top-ups and OA-to-SA transfers share `FRS − SA − active CPFIS-SA`; voluntary 3-account contributions (`vc3aAnnualRoom`) respect the $37,740 statutory CPF Annual Limit after mandatory OW + AW contributions. Monthly extra CPF savings blocked by legal limits remain in an outside reserve (default 0% return, never CPF's 4%) and transfer when room opens.
- **Salary, Additional Wage (AW) bonus & mortgage modes:** Uses 2026 and announced 2027 CPF age-band tables including senior `65–70` (16.5% total / 7.5% employee) and `>70` (12.5% total / 5% employee) bands, $8,000/month Ordinary Wage ceiling (`C.wageCap`) and `$102,000 − Total OW` Additional Wage ceiling (`C.annualWageCap`). Supports 4 housing OA modes (`All new OA`, `HDB Loan 2.6%`, `Bank Mortgage`, and `Paid-Up $0 OA`) with optional co-owner split and remaining loan tenure (`housingYears`).
- **IRAS YA 2024+ tax relief & SRS:** Models progressive resident income tax brackets, the $80,000 total personal relief cap, $8,000 self cash RSTU + $8,000 family cash RSTU, and $15,300/yr SRS cap. Both `taxSweetSpot()` and `firePlan()` allow SRS contributions up to `retire` (prior to the first penalty-free withdrawal at `Math.max(srsLockInAge, retire)` that starts the 10-year 50%-taxable drawdown window), account for ongoing SRS returns during the 10-year drawdown, and record **net post-tax** SRS draw (`netSrsDraw`) in `monthlyTotalIncome` with `monthlySrsTax` tracked separately.
- **Multi-bucket FIRE, 4-Tap non-overlap & longevity horizon:** Models distinct outside buckets (Global Equities, SG Dividends, tiered HYSA/MAS SSB/T-Bill cash accounts with $200k SSB cap overflow to 6M T-Bills, SRS, and withdrawable post-55 excess CPF OA) across a configurable longevity horizon from age 80 to 105 (default age 95 in the planner; legacy baseline `C.endAge = 90` in raw single-pot helpers). In `retirementTapsPlan()`, Tap 3 owns liquid equities only and Tap 4 owns SSB/Cash + bond sleeve so Tap 3 + Tap 4 equals total liquid outside assets with zero overlap. When a plan is already funded through `endAge`, `firePlan().fireNumber.requiredInvestTo95` solves downward to the true minimum monthly investment (`$0` if already self-sustaining).
- **CPF LIFE & spending presets:** Payouts interpolate from published 2026 male Standard-plan RA-at-65 anchors scaled by `cohortFRS / 220400` (capped at the highest reference anchor). Escalating plan starts at ~80% and rises 2% on payout anniversaries.
- **Today's purchasing power:** Underlying cash flows are nominal; displayed future amounts are discounted by `(1 + inflation)^years` to the event date.

## Model Boundaries & Excluded Items

- **Assumptions:** Current rates held beyond announced periods, 3.5% FRS growth after 2027, 5% BHS growth after 2026 (fixed at age 65), constant after-fee returns, and flat real salary (no career breaks or real wage step-ups unless manually updated).
- **Excluded costs & proceeds:** Property purchase price/downpayment, lease decay monetization (Lease Buyback / Silver Housing Bonus), stamp duties, moving costs, means-tested Silver Support / Workfare grants, and individual medical underwriting loadings.
- **Not personalised pricing:** Payouts above the highest cohort-scaled anchor are capped and labelled as a model reference boundary rather than a statutory maximum.
